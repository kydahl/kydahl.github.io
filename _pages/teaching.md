## Teaching Experience

I have extensive experience teaching mathematics at both undergraduate and graduate levels, focusing on **applied mathematics, calculus, and mathematical modeling**. My approach emphasizes **active learning, interdisciplinary applications, and real-world problem-solving**. I have taught courses and mentored students at **Virginia Tech, the University of Georgia, Purdue University, and the University of Hawai‘i at Mānoa**.

### **Other Pedagogical Contributions**
Beyond classroom teaching, I try to contribute to mathematical education and pedagogy. I recently co-authored an article for *MAA Focus* with **Stacy Potes**, professor of education at the **University of Hawai‘i at Mānoa**, titled *"The Proliferation of Place-Based Math Pedagogy in Hawai‘i,"* set to appear in the April/May 2025 issue. This article describes innovative approaches to mathematics education that integrate cultural and environmental contexts used by math educators in Hawaiʻi.
