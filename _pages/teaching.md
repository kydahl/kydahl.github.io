## Teaching Experience

I have taught courses in applied mathematics, calculus, and mathematical modeling at Virginia Tech, the University of Georgia, Purdue University, and the University of Hawai‘i at Mānoa. My teaching emphasizes active learning, interdisciplinary applications, and real-world problem-solving.

### Current Teaching
- **Instructor**, *Linear Analysis*, Cal Poly (Fall 2026)

## Outreach

Beyond classroom teaching, I actively contribute to mathematical education and outreach. I co-authored an article with **Stacy Potes**, professor of education at the **University of Hawai‘i at Mānoa**, for *MAA Focus* titled *"The Proliferation of Place-Based Math Pedagogy in Hawai‘i,"* set to appear in the April/May 2025 issue.
