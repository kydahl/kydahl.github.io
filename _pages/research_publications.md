## Research Overview

My research focuses on mathematical models that describe the transmission dynamics of mosquito-borne diseases. I use dynamical systems, data-driven approaches, and temperature-dependent models to investigate disease outbreaks and inform public health strategies. My work integrates theoretical analysis with empirical data to improve our understanding of disease ecology.

### Current Projects

- **Temperature and Disease Transmission:** Exploring the role of temperature variation in mosquito population dynamics and disease spread.
- **Vector-Borne Disease Models:** Developing mathematical models to describe the interactions between host, vector, and pathogen.
- **Data-Driven Parameter Estimation:** Applying statistical and computational techniques to calibrate models against real-world data.
